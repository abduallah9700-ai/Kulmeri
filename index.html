Kulmeri Academic Syllabus

Barista Training Curriculum

The Barista Training program at Kulmeri Academic takes you from beginner basics to commercial coffee shop mastery. Over 4 comprehensive weeks, you receive extensive hands-on station time with guidance from SCA-certified baristas.

Week 1: Coffee Origins, Bean Chemistry & Espresso Extraction Module 1

Arabica vs Robusta, bean processing methods, grinder calibration, dose-to-yield brew ratios, and dialing in espresso shots.

Week 2: Milk Science & Latte Art Foundation Module 2

Milk texturing and creating silky microfoam, pitcher ergonomics, basic pouring mechanics: Heart, Tulip, and Rosetta designs.

Week 3: Manual Brewing & Specialty Beverage Menu Module 3

Mastery of V60, Chemex, Aeropress, French Press, cold brew immersion, signature iced drinks, and syrups.

Week 4: Commercial Workflow, Speed Bar & Machine Maintenance Module 4

High-volume rush hour simulation, order taking, grouphead backflushing, steam wand sanitation, and final certification exam.

Kulmeri Professional Barista Certificate

Kulmeri Academic Syllabus

Pastry Training Curriculum

The Pastry Training program at Kulmeri Academic is an intensive 6-week masterclass covering baking essentials, artisan viennoiserie, sponge crafting, and delicate dessert artistry inside a professional commercial kitchen.

Week 1-2: French Viennoiserie & Artisan Yeast Breads Module 1

Lamination butter folding, flaky croissants, pains au chocolat, brioche rolls, Danish pastries, and artisan bread shaping.

Week 3-4: Sponge Baking, Layering & Cake Finishing Module 2

Vanilla & chocolate génoise, Swiss meringue buttercream, level crumb coating, berry coulis fillings, and sharp edges.

Week 5: Tarts, Choux Pastry & Confectionery Module 3

Pâte sablée, fruit tarts, chocolate ganache tarts, éclairs, cream puffs with craquelin, and delicate macarons.

Week 6: Chocolate Tempering, Mirror Glazes & Bakery Business Module 4

Seeding chocolate, mirror glaze drip cakes, dessert presentation, commercial bakery costing, and graduation showcase.

Certified Kulmeri Pastry Specialist
